Things move in the library for two reasons: to show where something came from or went (a Dialog opening, an Accordion unfolding, a Toast arriving), and to show that work is under way (a Spinner, a Skeleton's shimmer). Nothing moves for decoration.

State changes and overlays (a Button's hover, a Popover opening, a Tab being selected) use `--duration-sm`, so the interface answers at once. The longest role is for loops: a Skeleton's shimmer and an indeterminate Progress bar.

## Easing

<EaseCurves />

Each curve runs from the start of a movement (bottom left) to its end (top right). `--ease-out` starts fast and settles, for things arriving; `--ease-in` starts slowly and leaves fast, for things going; `--ease-in-out` does both, for things moving on screen. `--ease-bounce` overshoots and settles back, and only a Switch uses it.

The build checks that no duration is shorter than the one before it, and that each curve is one CSS can run.

## Opening and closing

Dialogs, Popovers, Tooltips and Toasts fade and grow in as they appear, and shrink and fade as they go; a dismissed Banner fades out. None of it needs a script. The browser animates them into and out of the top layer, and a Dialog's backdrop fades with it.

## Less motion

- **Reduced motion.** When a reader asks for less motion, every duration role becomes 0, so things appear and disappear at once. A Spinner is the exception: it slows to one turn every two seconds rather than stopping, because a still Spinner looks like something broke.
- **Reduced transparency.** Translucent, blurred surfaces become solid, so text behind glass never shows through.
- **High contrast.** See [Colour](/docs/foundations/colour/#high-contrast).

## Using motion

- **Use the duration roles,** never a length in your own CSS, so reduced motion stops your transitions too.
- **Move only to explain.** Movement should show where something came from, or that something is happening.
- **Never move anything across the page on its own,** and never flash. Content that moves, blinks or scrolls by itself for more than five seconds needs a way to pause it (WCAG 2.2.2).
