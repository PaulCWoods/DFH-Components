Design For Humans is built in the open, and so are the conversations about it. Ask in public rather than in private, and the answer helps everyone who has the same question after you.

## Ask, suggest and share in Discussions

[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions) is the library's community space. Choose the category that fits:

- **[Q&A](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions/categories/q-a):** how to do something, or why something works the way it does. Mark the reply that answers you, so the next reader finds it first.
- **[Ideas](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions/categories/ideas):** a component, option or token you'd like, and the problem it solves for you. The problem matters more than the solution: it may already have an answer in the library.
- **[Show and tell](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions/categories/show-and-tell):** a site, theme or screen you've made with the library.
- **[General](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions/categories/general):** anything else.

[Announcements](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions/categories/announcements) carries news of each release.

## Report a bug in Issues

[Issues](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/issues) is for something that's broken: a component that looks or behaves wrong, a failed check, a mistake in these docs. Search first, since someone may have reported it already. A good report has:

- **The version** of the library you link (the number in its URL).
- **The browser and device,** and for an accessibility bug, the assistive technology and its version.
- **The smallest markup that shows it,** or a link to a page that does.
- **What you expected, and what happened.** A screenshot helps.

Not sure it's a bug? Ask in Q&A first. A discussion can become an issue once it's confirmed.

## Changing the library

Start with a discussion in Ideas before you write code. Every change follows the library's principles: one axis per class, Tier 2 tokens, native HTML before ARIA, and every check passing. Agreeing on the approach first saves you time. The repository's `README.md` says how to run it, and `AGENTS.md` lists every command and check.

## Keep it kind

Be patient and generous with each other, as you would at work. Many people here are learning, and some write in a second language.
