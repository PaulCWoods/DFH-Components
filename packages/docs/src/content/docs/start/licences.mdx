The code is free under the MIT licence, for any project. The Figma kit and Pro are paid, and these terms say what each one lets you do. They are written to be read: if something isn't clear, email [hello@designforhumans.blog](mailto:hello@designforhumans.blog).

## The Figma kit

You buy a licence once, and it includes every update to the kit for as long as the kit is sold.

| Licence | Who can use it |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Solo** | One person |
| **Team** | Up to 10 people in one organisation |
| **Unlimited** | Everyone in one organisation, agencies included |

### Who counts

A person counts when they open the kit file in Figma, or edit a file built with its components. People who only view, comment on or inspect the designs, in Dev Mode for example, don't count.

A licence belongs to whoever bought it, a person or an organisation. When someone leaves your team, their place can go to someone else.

### What you can do

- **Use the kit in any number of projects,** your own and your clients', commercial ones included.
- **Hand over design files built with it** as part of a project. A client who wants the kit itself, to design with after your project, needs their own licence.
- **Change it as you like:** apply your own theme, recolour it, rename things, add components.
- **Build the designs in code** with the free library, under its MIT licence.

### What you can't do

- **Share the kit file,** or a changed copy of it: no reselling, giving it away, or posting it publicly or in the Figma Community.
- **Put it in something others buy or download,** such as a UI kit, a template, a theme or a design system, in whole or in part.
- **Stretch a licence past its number of people:** a Solo licence is for one person, and a Team licence for up to 10.

## Pro

Pro is a subscription for each editor: the person who uses the Token Designer's Pro features. Each editor has their own licence key and keeps it to themselves. The Agency plan is one key shared by up to five editors in one organisation: it works in a limited number of browsers. Removing Pro from a browser frees its place: in the Designer's menu, or in your Polar customer portal for a browser you can't open any more.

- **What you make is yours.** Your themes, and the CSS and Figma files they build, are yours to use and license however you like.
- **Cancel at any time.** Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. After that your themes stay in your browser and in the files you downloaded, and the free Designer still opens them.

## Refunds

If the kit or Pro isn't right for you, email [hello@designforhumans.blog](mailto:hello@designforhumans.blog) within 14 days of buying for a full refund. You don't need to give a reason.

## Payment and receipts

[Polar](https://polar.sh) sells the kit and Pro for us. It takes the payment, adds any VAT or sales tax due where you are, and sends your receipt and invoice. Your licence keys and downloads stay in Polar's customer portal, linked from the receipt.
