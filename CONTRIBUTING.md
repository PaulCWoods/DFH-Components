# Get involved

Design For Humans is built in the open, and so are the conversations about it. Ask in public, and the answer helps everyone who has the same question after you.

- **Questions, ideas and things you've made:** [Discussions](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/discussions). Use Q&A for questions (mark the reply that answers you), Ideas for a component, option or token you'd like, and Show and tell for your work.
- **Bugs:** [Issues](https://github.com/PaulCWoods/DFH-Components/issues). Say which version of the library you link, the browser and device (and assistive technology, for an accessibility bug), the smallest markup that shows it, and what you expected. Not sure it's a bug? Ask in Q&A first.
- **Changes to the library:** start with a discussion in Ideas before you write code, so we agree on the approach. Every change follows `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` and passes the checks in `AGENTS.md`.

[Get involved](https://components.designforhumans.blog/docs/start/contributing/) in the docs says more.
